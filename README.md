## Project Overview

This project analyses 14 days of sales and stock data from Wanjiku's vegetable stall at Gikomba Market. The aim is to use Python to understand sales performance, identify daily and weekly sales patterns, compare weekday and weekend performance, identify products requiring restocking, and calculate gross margins. The findings are used to provide practical business recommendations to help Wanjiku improve stock management and make informed business decisions.

## Top Three Findings

1. **Week-on-week sales growth:** Total sales increased from KES 22,050 in Week 1 to KES 24,400 in Week 2, representing a growth of approximately 10.66%.

2. **Weekend sales performance:** Average weekend sales were KES 4,900, compared to KES 2,685 on weekdays. Weekend sales were approximately 82.50% higher.

3. **Stock management:** Tomatoes and sukuma were below their reorder levels and need restocking.
